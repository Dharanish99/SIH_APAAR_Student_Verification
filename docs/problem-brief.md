## Problem in our own words
The supplied brief describes uneven institutional reporting: some student-level submissions and some aggregate counts. The proposed platform should connect admission records to APAAR and support validation, monitoring and authorised verification.

Our research must establish the real workflows, authoritative evidence, available integrations and applicable policies behind this proposal.

## Scope baseline
1. Secure institutional upload and update of student-level records.
2. Approved intake, actual enrolment, supernumerary seats, NRI, OCI, Foreign National and relevant student fields.
3. Detection of duplicates, missing/invalid IDs, excess admission and inconsistent/potentially conflicting enrolments.
4. Institution and AICTE dashboards.
5. Authorised stakeholder verification.
6. Verifiable academic-identity-linked records.
7. Integration with institutional and AICTE systems.
8. Security, privacy, authentication, role-based access, audit trails and consent.
9. Regulatory, monitoring, accreditation and policy reports.
10. APAAR ID plus institutional student ID display/printing on certificates from awarding bodies.

The [requirements matrix](requirements.md) assigns research ownership. This paraphrase is a planning baseline, not a substitute for the official statement.

## Working distinctions to investigate
- An identifier's format is different from proof of identity.
- A recognised identity does not prove the admission or qualification.
- One person can have enrolment history; potential conflicts need context.
- Intake rules depend on authoritative approval, category, programme and academic year.
- Reporting authority and employer verification need separately justified permissions.
- Record authenticity needs a trusted source and integrity, not just a QR code.

## Open questions
- Which government interfaces and sandbox permissions are available to this team?
- Where do authoritative, versioned intake approvals come from?
- What is the appropriate workflow for students awaiting APAAR and foreign students?
- Who resolves disputed identity and enrolment records?
- What event/update frequency is realistically meant by real-time monitoring?
- Which policy amendments and data protection provisions apply to the intended pilot?
