# Research and AI-use standards

## Claim types
| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified fact | Supported by inspected authoritative evidence, with scope and date |
| Observation | What a documented interview or experiment actually showed |
| Assumption | A provisional input that could be wrong |
| Proposal | A design recommendation, not an existing capability |
| Unknown | Evidence is missing or conflicting |

Confidence describes evidence strength, not certainty. A seed link, search snippet or AI answer is not enough to mark a consequential claim verified.

## Source discipline
Prefer official policy, documentation and first-party technical specifications. Use secondary sources to discover primary ones or label their limitations.
Record source ID, claim, URL, publisher, version/publication date if known, access date, exact section and applicability.
Use `unknown` for an unknown date; never invent one.
Check amendments, commencement dates, course/institution scope and admission year.
Separate original text from your interpretation. Preserve contradictory evidence and resolve it explicitly.
Do not reproduce entire copyrighted documents in the repository; use links and short necessary excerpts.

## Interviews and observations
Record date, role, method and limitations. Obtain permission before recording or attributing quotes.
Prefer anonymised notes. A few interviews are qualitative evidence, not a national prevalence estimate.
Ask about actual recent tasks, error corrections and blank templates. Do not ask for private exports.

## AI-assisted workflow
1. Frame a specific question and get a plain-language explanation.
2. Find and open original sources.
3. Verify the relevant passage, date, applicability and exceptions.
4. Explain the result yourself.
5. Apply it to a worked case.
6. Ask AI to challenge the interpretation; check the challenge against sources.
7. Record the conclusion, uncertainty and implication.

Use AI for discovery, explanation, comparison, draft questions, synthetic cases and critique.
Never accept invented endpoints, false citations, unrun benchmarks or fabricated interviews.
Agreement between models is not independent confirmation.
Disclose meaningful AI assistance in the PR, and name human verification actually performed.

## Data and experimental integrity
This is a public repository. Use fictional data and clearly label fixtures and generated service responses.
No real APAAR/Aadhaar numbers, identifiable admission records, private phone/email details, credentials or restricted exports.
Document seeds, dependencies, commands, input size, inserted issues, measurements and limitations.
Synthetic results demonstrate constructed cases, not national deployment or real-world fraud accuracy.

## Understanding and review
Every member must explain the normal path, two failure cases, supporting evidence, biggest limitation and recommended tradeoff.
Reviewers check substance and applicability; grammar alone is not research review.
Only record human approval after it actually occurs.
