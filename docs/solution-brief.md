# SetuTrace - solution brief

Working name; an AI-authored recommendation for human review, 27 October 2026.

SetuTrace is a reporting and correction layer beside an institution's existing ERP. It turns a submitted batch into a traceable, institution-approved snapshot; explains each unresolved finding with its source and applicable rule; and follows corrections through to updated reports and verification receipts.

A staff member imports an existing export, previews changes, resolves actionable exceptions and requests approval. A student can see the facts shared about them and raise a correction through an authenticated or assisted route. A regulator reviews scoped submissions and internal conflict cases. An authorised verifier receives only the permitted current institution assertion. Missing APAAR or provider downtime leaves linkage pending while the institution can still capture and correct its record.

The useful difference to test is **less repeated reconciliation and more accountable correction**. Samarth already documents student import/edit operations, and NAD already supports digital academic awards. This proposal must prove that the extra evidence/correction workflow earns its operational cost. A structured spreadsheet/checklist remains a valid simpler alternative if the pilot finds little need for versioning and multi-party review.

The MVP uses fictional data, one narrow programme/cohort, staged CSV, deterministic checks, a correction case, an approval snapshot, role-scoped dashboards, an authorised online assertion and a synthetic certificate template. Government responses and issuer identities are labelled simulations. Live provider connections, cross-institution matching, real policy packs and actual certificates are separate gates.

Evidence supports the constraints, not yet the product's benefit. The token-linked APAAR route is documented; arbitrary bulk checking is not. Policy depends on course/year/authority and can allow concurrent programmes. A 1,000-row fictional model was reproduced, but no app, database race test, real student study or government call has been performed.
