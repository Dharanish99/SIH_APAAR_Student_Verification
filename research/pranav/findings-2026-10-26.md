## Recommendation

Build an evidence-backed review workflow around **versioned institution/course/year approvals and time-bounded enrolment events**. Keep identity checks, record quality, capacity arithmetic and legal admission decisions separate. A student appearing at two institutions is a reason to investigate context, not an automatic accusation. The platform should preserve the record and explain which evidence is missing instead of silently rejecting a student.

This research supports R02 and R03 directly and contributes to R01, R04, R08, R09 and R10. ## Interpretation of critical exceptions

**A repeated identifier is not a policy conclusion.** First decide whether the inputs describe the same row, a later revision, a historical enrolment, a transfer, or concurrently active programmes. For concurrent programmes, ask the designated reviewer for the relevant programme modes, class-time relationship and governing rules. Do not infer class conflicts from the words “full time.” UGC's June 2025 modification also changes the treatment of qualifying pre-guideline dual programmes; a simple admission-year cutoff would be wrong. These are document-supported distinctions, not a finding that any particular student's two degrees are valid. [PRA-103](https://www.ugc.gov.in/pdfnews/6493421_UGC-Guidelines-pursuing-two-academic-programmes-simultaneously.pdf)

**Status and seat are different facts.** “OCI,” “foreign national,” and “NRI” are not mutually interchangeable capacity buckets. A person's documentary status does not itself identify the seat against which the authority admitted them. The Maharashtra July 2026 amendment supplies a concrete example: certain pre-4-March-2021 OCI holders may qualify for state or all-India candidature under stated schooling/residence conditions. This example must not be promoted to a universal entitlement. Store the authority's category decision and its evidence; avoid collecting passport images merely to populate analytics. [PRA-109](https://dte.maharashtra.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Student-Transfer-Circular_2026-27-with-attachement.pdf)

**A transfer is an authorised lifecycle change.** The same DTE packet requires eligibility/vacancy evidence and has category-specific restrictions. Its current text supersedes an older autonomous-institution prohibition in the attached historical text. Neither “all transfers are invalid” nor “all transfers are automatically allowed” is defensible. The platform should join old and new enrolment events only after the relevant approvals, using explicit effective dates. Other states and universities need their own confirmed rules. [PRA-109](https://dte.maharashtra.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Student-Transfer-Circular_2026-27-with-attachement.pdf)

## Authority and correction design — proposal

1. **Approval intake:** An authorised regulator/policy administrator registers the authoritative EoA, corrigenda and applicable circulars. Store a document digest and source URL, institution/campus/course/year identifiers, extracted values, effective dates, reviewer and status. A digest proves consistency of the retained file, not its issuer authenticity. A second reviewer confirms material capacity changes before activation.
2. **Institution submission:** An operator imports a batch. The institution approver confirms joining/withdrawal facts and their supporting references. Store original values and each correction as a revision; do not overwrite the historical submission.
3. **Automated evaluation:** Run only rules whose applicability and required evidence are satisfied. Return `WITHIN_CONFIGURED_RULE`, `DATA_ERROR`, `POTENTIAL_CAPACITY_BREACH`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `POLICY_UNAVAILABLE`. None means a legal admission has been sanctioned.
4. **Dispute:** Show the student/institution an intelligible reason, disputed field, relevant rule version, evidence needed, owner and next action. Provide a correction/appeal case with a receipt. Proposed response-time targets belong to the pilot plan, not an invented statutory deadline.
5. **Resolution:** The designated institution or competent admission authority decides matters within its powers; AICTE handles its approval decisions. Record decision, authority, evidence, reason, effective date and superseded finding. Recompute affected aggregates and invalidate outdated verification results. Do not grant a reviewer authority merely because the application has an admin role.
6. **Cross-institution cases:** All accepted batches receive uniform receipts. Cross-institution collision cases remain internal to the lawfully designated reviewer, with minimum necessary evidence. A college may be asked to clarify its own supplied facts; do not reveal a match bit, another institution's record, or a special collision-only ticket whose existence exposes a match. This incorporates the security agent's AI critique.

## Proposed field dictionary

These are project fields, not a claim about a provider's API schema.

| Field group | Essential concepts | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Approval | institution/campus IDs, course ID/name mapping, programme, level, academic year, approval revision, approval status, base intake, approved pool caps, scope and effective dates | Prevent wrong-campus, wrong-year and stale-policy decisions. |
| Enrolment | local student key, identity reference/status, institution, course, admission cohort, current study year, entry route, programme mode, effective start/end, lifecycle state | Separate person from their educational history. |
| Category | declared nationality/status, evidenced status, admitted seat pool, authority decision reference | Avoid converting nationality directly into a seat. Values can be unknown or under review. |
| Review | finding ID, rule version, input revision, missing evidence, proposed outcome, authorised decision, student dispute, reason and timestamps | Make a decision reproducible and contestable. |
| Reporting | snapshot cutoff, received time, effective time, reporting period, scope, approved versus reported totals, unresolved counts | Prevent misleading “real-time” or stale aggregate comparisons. |

## Alternatives and trade-offs

| Approach | Benefit | Failure mode | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| One global quota table and duplicate blacklist | Small implementation | Wrong programme/year/category; penalises legitimate history and dual programmes | Reject. |
| Pure manual review | Handles nuance with little software | Inconsistent explanations and large review effort are plausible risks, not measured in this project | Keep as fallback and baseline for pilot evaluation. |
| Versioned rules plus human exception handling | Reproducible checks with room for evidence and correction | Policy maintenance and review ownership are essential | Preferred narrow MVP proposal. |
| ML predicts admission legality | Can prioritise unusual patterns | No trustworthy legal ground-truth dataset; opaque adverse decisions | Exclude from legal decisions. Optional queue ranking only after demonstrated added value and fairness review. |

## What remains external or uncertain

- The current complete 2026-27 AICTE addendum/corrigendum set was **not** retrieved from an authoritative publication endpoint. Discovery references suggest material changes. Historical 15% international-seat assumptions are therefore not an active rule in this package. Obtain the authoritative amendment and confirm all affected clauses before live use.
- No actual pilot institution's EoA, relevant affiliation, current seat matrix or special/court order was validated. The reopened Hisar document is a structural example, not a pilot approval. Pune's PDF could not be reopened and is not relied on for an operative conclusion.
- The complete matrix of state, university, course and professional-council exceptions is not claimed. Narrow the first pilot to one institution/course/year/jurisdiction and have its rules approved.
- Reporting provisions reviewed do not prove that every field in the proposed platform can lawfully be collected, shared with employers, or searched across institutions. Sudhir must define purpose and access decisions.
- Missing APAAR and foreign-student pathways remain provider/policy dependencies. The safe design is provisional data capture with a visible pending reason; that design does not create a right to admission.
- No interviews, human policy review, live government validation, field trial or product test occurred in this workstream. The 22 worked cases are reasoned synthetic examples, not measured model accuracy.
