# Versioned rule catalogue — proposed evaluation design

Prepared by the policy research agent, 2026-10-26. These rules are design specifications, not executable government policy. `base-edition-only` means the inspected 2024-27 APH passage is known but current amendments/applicability are not completely verified. Such a rule must not issue a production legal decision. The authoritative [source register](sources.csv) supplies URLs and access details.

Every rule instance requires: `rule_id`, version, jurisdiction, institution types, programmes/levels, entry routes, applicable academic years, effective interval, authoritative sources/clauses, supersedes, required inputs, evaluation, outcome semantics, exception route, reviewer and activation status. Store each evaluation with input revision, rule revision and reporting cutoff.

## Rule activation and changes

Draft → source checked → applicability checked → synthetic cases reviewed → authorised policy approval → active. This is proposed governance. It is separate from an application's technical deployment. New circulars/court orders pause affected rules or create a new version; they do not rewrite historical evaluations silently. Re-evaluation records both old and new outcomes and notifies the responsible case owner when the change matters.

If the platform lacks an authoritative source, evaluate data quality and preserve the record while reporting `POLICY_UNAVAILABLE`. A human may inspect the case; an agent may not invent a quota to finish a dashboard.
