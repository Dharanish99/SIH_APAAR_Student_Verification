# Threat model and security evaluation plan

**Author:** AI security researcher supporting Sudhir. **Research round:** 2026-10-26, Asia/Kolkata. **Completion recheck:** 2026-10-27. **Evidence boundary:** design analysis only; no implementation, penetration test, live provider call or human security approval.

## Scope and assets

Protect student identity links, institution-attested enrolment revisions, restricted overlap evidence, disclosure grants, issuer keys, provider tokens, audit records and correction rights. Attackers include an unauthenticated visitor, dishonest verifier, malicious or compromised institution user, colluding insiders and an attacker controlling an upload or callback. Honest mistakes and outages also harm students.

Trust boundaries are browser/API; API/tenant-scoped database and files; API/queue workers; institution/restricted matching service; service/authorised provider; issuer/verifier; and operations/logs/backups. Authentication alone does not establish purpose, tenant ownership or issuer mandate. The [permission matrix](privacy-design.md#permission-matrix) defines the proposed policy.

## Security invariants

1. Knowing APAAR, a local ID or QR URL reveals no student data or existence by itself.
2. Grant and record revision are checked at disclosure time, not only link creation.
3. Ordinary institutions see their own claims and authorised decisions. Cross-institution discovery needs assigned review authority; matching results are not returned to uploaders.
4. Format validity, institution attestation, provider evidence and review resolution are separate facts. One never silently upgrades another.
5. Mismatch, overlap, missing APAAR and provider outage produce an explainable pending/review path, never automatic fraud or invalid-admission findings.
6. Correction preserves provenance without exposing dispute details. Suspending a digital credential for key compromise does not itself invalidate the underlying academic award.
7. Recovery cannot restore revoked grants or bypass a deletion/hold decision. Retained evidence remains restricted.

## Evidence and validation boundary

[OWASP authorisation](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html), [logging](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html) and [upload](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/File_Upload_Cheat_Sheet.html) guidance inform T01/T04/T09. [OAuth BCP](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9700.html) and [JWT BCP](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8725.html) inform T05/T07. [W3C VC 2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) and [status lists](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-bitstring-status-list/) explain credential limits. These sources do not attest to this proposed service's security; inspected versions/sections are in [sources.csv](sources.csv).

This is a test plan, not a test report. No risk percentage, anonymity guarantee or passed security test is claimed. A future pilot needs independent assessment and the [pilot gates](privacy-design.md#pilot-entry-and-exit-gates). A confirmed critical event requires stopping affected disclosure, containment, restricted evidence preservation, incident-owner involvement, applicable reporting, correction and documented reopening review.
